Windmill

Mixed licence AGPLv3 core with Apache 2.0 clients/spec and proprietary enterprise/community restrictions

Collected
2026-11-18

Research-based — not yet hands-on tested

Where it is ranked

  • Back office: rank 5, score 84.0. Windmill runs scripts in multiple languages as jobs, chains them into flows, exposes apps and APIs, and provides secrets, data tables, and scheduling for internal operations.
  • Reporting and content: rank 6, score 84.0. Windmill AI Agent steps can process data, generate content, execute scripts, and make decisions inside automated workflows, with providers and models configured as resources.

Owner fit

Setup
Docker Compose starts server, workers, PostgreSQL, and LSP; Kubernetes/Helm is available, and the documented minimum is 2 CPU cores, 4 GB RAM, and 20 GB disk. Sources: windmill.dev
Approval
yes — flow approval steps suspend execution until approval/resume, and AI-agent tool calls can require approval before running. Sources: windmill.dev, windmill.dev
Data
Self-hosting keeps data on your machines and secrets in your network according to the self-hosting page; the architecture uses your server, workers, and PostgreSQL, with optional external AI providers receiving prompts/data according to configured use. Sources: windmill.dev, windmill.dev
Cost
Community Edition is free with unlimited executions; pricing seen 2026-11-18 lists Enterprise from $120/month plus seat/compute pricing, including $170/month example base and $20/month per builder. Sources: windmill.dev
Community
18,608 GitHub stars (2026-11-18). Sources: api.github.com

Licence

Mixed licence AGPLv3 core with Apache 2.0 clients/spec and proprietary enterprise/community restrictions. The LICENSE assigns backend/frontend core to AGPLv3 except enterprise-gated snippets, clients and OpenAPI to Apache 2.0, and adds proprietary terms to Community Edition binaries/images that restrict selling, managed service, modification, wrapping, and re-exposure.
