Where it is ranked
  • Lead follow-up: rank 13, score 75.0. Comp AI CRM runs a background agent that turns a new person on a mailbox thread into a contact record, researches and enriches the contact and company from the team's own mail and meetings, writes strongly evidenced facts to the record, leaves weaker evidence as suggestions a person settles, and schedules its own re-checks; it reads mail only and never sends.
    Score breakdown: Research evidence 50 × 0.35; Owner fit 75 × 0.3; Project health 100 × 0.25; Licence clarity 100 × 0.1.
Owner fit
Setup
Bun and Docker: clone the repo, fill .env (auth secret, sign-in allow-list, a Google or Microsoft OAuth client), start Postgres with docker compose, apply migrations and run; production is three deployments (Next.js app, NestJS API, agent) plus Postgres. Sources: github.com
Approval
yes — the agent writes only strongly evidenced facts; weaker evidence becomes a suggestion that a person settles, and the connections page requires review of new or changed agent tools before use (documented in the repository, no paid tier is described). Sources: github.com, github.com
Data
Single-tenant deployment on the operator's own Postgres; the agent sandbox has deny-all network egress and is never given DATABASE_URL, and every outside research source is optional and off unless its key is set; models are reached through the Vercel AI Gateway. Sources: github.com
