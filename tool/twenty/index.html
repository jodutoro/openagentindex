Where it is ranked

  • Lead follow-up: rank 1, score 89.3. Twenty is a CRM organized around companies, people and opportunities, with workflows, REST/GraphQL APIs, webhooks and AI agents that can automate CRM follow-up tasks.

Owner fit

Setup
The official production guide uses Docker Compose, requires at least 2 GB RAM, an environment file, encryption key, Postgres password and docker compose up -d. Sources: docs.twenty.com
Approval
yes — the Draft Email workflow step saves a generated email to the connected mailbox's Drafts folder for manual review and sending instead of sending it, and the Send to Inbox step asks a person for an answer or an approval. Both are in the workflow actions documentation with no plan restriction stated; the AGPL-3.0 core is free to self-host (SSO and row-level permissions are the paid items). Sources: docs.twenty.com, twenty.com
Data
Self-hosting keeps CRM data on the operator's infrastructure; AI providers and integrations must be configured separately, and the official pricing page says enterprise can use its own AI providers. Sources: docs.twenty.com, twenty.com
Cost
Self-hosting the AGPL-3.0 core is free; managed cloud starts at $9/user/month, Organization is $39/user/month, and Enterprise starts at $50,000/year (seen 2026-11-18). Sources: twenty.com

Licence

Mixed licence AGPLv3 with MIT packages and commercial enterprise files. The repository LICENSE applies AGPLv3 to most of the project and grants a Twenty Application Exception. Certain packages are MIT (the twenty CLI and SDKs, twenty-shared, the twenty-ui component library and the applications under packages/twenty-apps). Files marked with a /* @license Enterprise */ comment are under a commercial licence, defined at the end of the LICENSE file, which requires a Commercial Subscription for production use.
